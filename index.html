🚀 What Makes LGT Unique

LGT offers two distinct pool types for projects:

1. Standard Project Pools (single reward – $LGT)

Projects pay a creation fee (100% refunded after pool fills). Pool size tiers determine the $LGT multiplier for users:

  • $2,000 – $9,000 → 2x
  • $10,000 – $25,000 → 3x
  • $26,000 – $50,000 → 4x
  • $51,000 – $500,000 → 5x

Fund allocation from user deposits:

  • 20% → free marketing campaign for the project
  • 5% of remaining 80% → LGT team
  • 75% of entire pool → $LGT liquidity on listing day
2. Dual‑Token Reward Pool (project token + $LGT)

Projects contribute USDC + their own token. Users earn both $LGT and the project's token. Of total deposits:

  • 30% goes directly to the project (as revenue)
  • 40% automatically buys the project's token (creating buy pressure)
  • 30% adds to $LGT liquidity
